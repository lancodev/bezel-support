# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2, 2026_

This policy covers the Bezel plugin for Stream Deck ("Bezel"), published by Kyle Anderson ("I", "me").

## What Bezel collects

Nothing. Bezel does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking. It makes no network requests other than the local connection to the Stream Deck app running on your own computer.

## What stays on your computer

- **Settings:** the settings you choose for each key and for the plugin are stored by the Stream Deck app on your computer.
- **Logs:** Bezel writes diagnostic log files (for example, which monitor commands failed) to the `logs` folder inside the plugin folder on your computer. They never leave your computer unless you choose to share them, for example when reporting a problem.
- **Monitor communication:** Bezel talks to your monitor over the DDC/CI connection in your display cable. That communication stays between your computer and your monitor.

## Purchases

Bezel is sold through the Elgato Marketplace. Your account, payment and purchase information is handled by Elgato and its payment processor under their own privacy policies. I don't receive your payment details.

## Support requests

If you contact me through GitHub issues, the information you post is public and is handled by GitHub under its privacy policy. Please don't include personal information you don't want to be public. You can delete your own issue comments at any time, or ask me to remove an issue.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
